Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

Your logs are stored on your own device. If you create an optional account, a private backup is kept so you can restore after reinstalling or move to a new phone. See the Privacy Policy for the full detail.

How do I export or delete my data?

Open Settings → Backup and tap Export to save a copy of everything. To erase it, use Settings → Danger zone → Clear all data. If you have an account, Delete account in the Community tab removes everything you have shared from our servers as well.

Something I logged is missing. Can I get it back?

Open Settings and look for a Recover lost data card. It appears only when a safety copy exists, and restoring only adds records back — it never overwrites anything newer. If the card is not there, email us and we will help.

How are my calorie and macro targets calculated?

They come from published, peer-reviewed methods, not guesswork. Tap Settings → Science & sources in the app to see the exact equations, the safety limits we apply, and links to every source. A short summary is on this page under Where our numbers come from.

What does the AI coach send off my device?

Only when you use it: your question, a compact summary of your recent stats, and any photo you explicitly submit. These go to our coach service and are processed by Anthropic's Claude to produce the answer, then discarded. You are asked to agree before anything is ever sent, and declining leaves every other feature working.

How do I cancel or manage a subscription?

Your first 30 days of the Premium AI features are included with the app — there is nothing to cancel. Any future subscription would be managed in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Do I need an account?

No. Every tracking and insight feature works without one. An account only adds the optional community and cloud backup.

Is OptimalFit medical advice?

No. OptimalFit is a fitness and wellness tool. Its insights are statistics computed from what you log, and its guidance is general fitness and nutrition information — not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Talk to a qualified healthcare professional before making significant changes to your diet or exercise, especially if you are pregnant, under 18, or have a medical condition. If you feel unwell while exercising, stop and seek medical help.

Where our numbers come from

Calorie and macronutrient targets in OptimalFit are derived from published methods and are bounded by widely used safety limits:

  • Resting energy expenditure — Mifflin-St Jeor equation. Find on PubMed
  • Daily energy needs and activity factors — Dietary Guidelines for Americans. dietaryguidelines.gov
  • Rate of weight change and calorie deficits — NIH Body Weight Planner. niddk.nih.gov/bwp
  • Protein intake for active people — International Society of Sports Nutrition position stand on protein and exercise. Find on PubMed
  • Physical activity — World Health Organization guidelines on physical activity and sedentary behaviour. who.int
  • Resistance-training volume and progression — Schoenfeld, Ogborn & Krieger, dose-response of weekly volume and muscle mass. Find on PubMed

The complete list, with the specific numbers each source supports and how confident the evidence is, is inside the app under Settings → Science & sources.

OptimalFit applies safety floors regardless of what any calculation returns: targets are never set below 1,500 kcal/day for men or 1,200 kcal/day for women, and weight-change goals faster than 1% of body weight per week are flagged as unrealistic rather than followed.
